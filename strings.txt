PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX
	EN	Album Mix

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_DESC
	EN	Creates a continuous album-based mix from a seed album using Last.fm similarity.

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_CREATE
	EN	Create Album Mix

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_CREATING
	EN	Creating Album Mix...

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_STARTED
	EN	Album Mix started

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_QUEUED
	EN	Queued similar album: %s

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_NO_SIMILAR
	EN	No similar albums found

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_SETTINGS
	EN	Album Mix Settings

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_LASTFM_API_KEY
	EN	Last.fm API Key

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_LASTFM_API_KEY_DESC
	EN	Enter your Last.fm API key. Get one free at https://www.last.fm/api/account/create

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_MAX_HISTORY
	EN	Album History Size

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_MAX_HISTORY_DESC
	EN	Maximum number of albums remembered within one mix to avoid repeats (see also Don't Repeat Albums For).

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_ARTIST_COOLDOWN
	EN	Artist Cooldown (albums)

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_ARTIST_COOLDOWN_DESC
	EN	Number of albums that must be queued before the same artist can appear again. Set to 0 to disable. Default is 5.

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_LOOKAHEAD
	EN	Queue Lookahead (tracks)

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_LOOKAHEAD_DESC
	EN	Number of tracks before the end of the current album to queue the next one.

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_STOP
	EN	Stop Album Mix

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_STOPPED
	EN	Album Mix stopped

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_SEED_TRACK
	EN	Seed track: %s by %s

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_VARIETY
	EN	Variety

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_VARIETY_DESC
	EN	How many of the closest Last.fm matches to choose from at random (closer matches are still more likely). 1 always takes the closest match. Default is 10.

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_FILTER_COMPILATIONS
	EN	Skip Compilations

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_FILTER_COMPILATIONS_DESC
	EN	Don't queue greatest hits, best-of, collections, anthologies, soundtracks and similar albums. The album you start the mix from is always played.

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_FILTER_LIVE
	EN	Skip Live Albums

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_FILTER_LIVE_DESC
	EN	Don't queue live albums (e.g. "Live at ...", "In Concert", "Unplugged").

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_FILTER_SINGLES
	EN	Skip Singles and EPs

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_FILTER_SINGLES_DESC
	EN	Don't queue singles, EPs or remix releases, or albums with fewer tracks than the minimum below.

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_MIN_TRACKS
	EN	Minimum Tracks per Album

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_MIN_TRACKS_DESC
	EN	Albums with fewer tracks than this are treated as singles/EPs (only when Skip Singles and EPs is on, and only where the track count is known). 0 turns this off. Default is 5.

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_REPEAT_DAYS
	EN	Don't Repeat Albums For (days)

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_REPEAT_DAYS_DESC
	EN	An album queued by a mix won't be queued again for this many days. Remembered across mixes and server restarts, for all players together or for each player separately (see Saved History). 0 turns this off. Default is 30.

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_CLEAR_HISTORY
	EN	Clear Saved History

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_CLEAR_HISTORY_DESC
	EN	Tick and save to forget which albums have been played, so they can be queued again straight away. On this page it clears the shared history; a player's own history is cleared on that player's settings page.

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_ALBUMS_REMEMBERED
	EN	Albums remembered

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_DEFAULTS_NOTE
	EN	The settings below are the defaults for all players. Each player can use its own settings instead: Settings > Player > Album Mix.

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_SOURCE
	EN	Source

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_SOURCE_DESC
	EN	Where the mix looks for albums. "Online only" only queues albums that are not in your local library (discovery). The album you start the mix from is always played, wherever it comes from.

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_SOURCE_LIBRARY_ONLY
	EN	Library only

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_SOURCE_LIBRARY_FIRST
	EN	Library first, then online

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_SOURCE_ONLINE_FIRST
	EN	Online first, then library

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_SOURCE_ONLINE_ONLY
	EN	Online only (albums you don't own)

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_HISTORY_SCOPE
	EN	Saved History

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_HISTORY_SCOPE_DESC
	EN	Whether all players share one history of played albums (an album played in one room isn't repeated in another), or each player keeps its own. Synced players use the history of the main player.

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_HISTORY_SHARED
	EN	Shared by all players

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_HISTORY_PLAYER
	EN	Separate for each player

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_PLAYER_DESC
	EN	Album Mix settings for this player. The Last.fm API key, album history size, repeat days and saved history are set for the whole server under Settings > Advanced > Album Mix.

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_PLAYER_SYNCED
	EN	This player is synced: while it is, the settings of the main player in the group are used.

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_OWN_SETTINGS
	EN	Use Own Settings for This Player

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_OWN_SETTINGS_DESC
	EN	When ticked, this player uses the settings below. When not ticked, it uses the server defaults under Settings > Advanced > Album Mix, and the values below are kept but not used.

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_CLEAR_PLAYER_HISTORY_DESC
	EN	Tick and save to forget which albums this player has played, so they can be queued again straight away.

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_CREATE_ARTIST

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_QUEUED_LIKE_ARTIST
	EN	Queued %s, similar artist to %s

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_NO_API_KEY
	EN	Album Mix: no Last.fm API key set in the Album Mix settings

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_INCLUDE_SEED
	EN	Play the Starting Album or Track

PLUGIN_ALBUM_MIX_INCLUDE_SEED_DESC
	EN	When ticked, the album, track or artist you start a mix from is played first. When not ticked, the mix starts straight away with the first similar album. "Continue as Album Mix" always keeps what is playing.
